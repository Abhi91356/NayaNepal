# NayaNepal Core Team & Governance 🏛️

This document outlines the organizational structure, roles, and responsibilities for the NayaNepal enterprise platform.

## 1. Minimal Launch Team (Phase 0)
*For a lean, efficient MVP launch, the following core roles are established:*

*   **Product Owner**: Defines features, validates national needs, and owns the product backlog.
*   **Android Lead (Kotlin/Compose)**: Architecture and implementation of the multi-module Super App.
*   **Backend Lead (Node.js/Go)**: Design and deployment of scalable microservices and API Gateway.
*   **UI/UX Designer**: Branding, premium visual assets, and localized Nepali interfaces.
*   **QA Engineer**: Automated unit/integration testing and national-scale load testing.
*   **DevOps Engineer**: Kubernetes orchestration, CI/CD pipelines, and real-time monitoring.

---

## 2. Enterprise Scale Org Chart (Target State)

### 🚀 Product Leadership
*   **Chief Product Officer (CPO)**: Owns the 10-20 year national roadmap and user experience strategy.
*   **Domain Experts**:
    *   **Finance Lead**: Oversees Wallet & Payment Rail integration.
    *   **Agri Lead**: Strategy for Smart Agriculture Advisor.
    *   **Education Lead**: Vocational learning pathways.
    *   **Health Lead**: Telehealth & Health Records compliance.

### 🛠️ Engineering
*   **Tech Architect**: Oversees cloud-native system design, microservices, and API Gateway.
*   **Infrastructure (DevOps)**: Manages CI/CD pipelines, Kubernetes, and Cloud Storage.
*   **QA/Automation Lead**: Defines testing strategies across all 15+ modules.

### ⚖️ Operations & Governance
*   **Compliance & Legal**: Ensures national data residency and privacy compliance.
*   **Partnerships Manager**: Coordinates with the Ministry of ICT, Nepal Rastra Bank, and NGOs.

## 3. RACI Matrix (Responsibility Assignment)

| Key Decisions / Tasks | Product Owner | Tech Architect | Dev Leads | Compliance | Partners |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Product Roadmap** | **A/R** | C | C | I | C |
| **System Architecture** | I | **A/R** | C | C | I |
| **Security Protocols** | I | **A/R** | R | **A** | I |
| **Data Privacy Policy** | C | I | I | **A/R** | C |
| **Gov/Bank Integration** | C | C | I | R | **A/R** |
| **Mobile UI/UX Design** | R | I | **A/R** | I | I |

**Legend**:
- **R**: Responsible (Does the work)
- **A**: Accountable (The decision maker)
- **C**: Consulted (Provides expertise)
- **I**: Informed (Updated on progress)
